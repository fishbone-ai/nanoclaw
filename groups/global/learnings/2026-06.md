## 2026-06-07 - Meeting: FitBot — AI Crisis Guardrail Discussion
- AI health/wellness agents can get drawn into mental health crisis conversations even without explicit product intent — users signal distress in ways that shift the conversation toward crisis management
- When an AI engages in a self-harm / mental health crisis conversation, it creates real risk of harm; the correct policy is to redirect to human support immediately and not attempt to manage the crisis alone
- Parasocial AI relationships (where users open up deeply to the bot) make crisis escalation more likely — this should be treated as a product safety assumption, not an edge case
- The "redirect to a human" guardrail is a blocking enabler: no amount of product quality work matters if a user in crisis gets a chatbot response instead of a hotline

## 2026-06-05 - Meeting: Avishay × Kedmi — FitBot Strategy & Mentorship
- "Finding deeper gaps" = making multiple hard-to-replicate hops: (1) find a problem people *actively search for*, (2) talk to users and understand their real pain, (3) build a solution — only then apply tech; using new tech on an old problem is just "one hop" and is trivially copyable
- Pull > Push: if customers don't come to you organically, you're running a paid distribution treadmill forever — this is the structural difference between a sustainable business and an uphill battle
- B2C fitness app churn is 20–30%/month — nearly impossible to grow net users without massive top-of-funnel; this applies to any accountability/habit-change product
- Stickiness is a moat: products where leaving is costly (e.g. Wix — your business website is on it) have fundamentally different retention economics than lifestyle apps where users can leave without consequence
- MRR headline is misleading — a $45K/month B2C AI app may net much less after Facebook ads, influencer commission (15%), and LLM API costs; always model net margin, not gross revenue
- Runway pressure (3 months) distorts decision-making; solo founders should maintain a day job and think in years, not sprint to PMF under forced urgency
- "You are not your user" — founders systematically underestimate how irrational and non-technical their actual users are; the correct response to "why don't they just learn?" is always "because they won't"
- WhatsApp AI bots create parasocial relationship risk — users develop emotional attachments to AI coaches; founders should decide upfront whether this is a feature or a liability

## 2026-06-08 - Meeting: Cybersecurity Exploration — AI Agent Memory & New Attack Surfaces
- Wiz (קוי) playbook: identify new technology creating novel attack surfaces before enterprises recognize the risk → validate with real breach evidence → build a product → sell to enterprise security buyers; this "enterprise blindness" framing is key
- Enterprise AI agent memory is an un-governed data store: most Claude/LLM agent memory is just Markdown files or vector DBs with no access controls — organizations running agentic workflows haven't thought about poisoning/injection of this "organizational brain"
- The right entry question for a cyber exploration is NOT "what do CISOs pay for today?" but rather "what new technology is entering enterprise use where the attack surface is novel and not yet mapped?"
- New cybersecurity companies consistently form at new technology verticals — AI is both a vertical (target of attacks) and a solution (defender); this creates multiple distinct startup opportunities in the same wave
- Memory poisoning via prompt injection (e.g. malicious emails processed by agentic pipelines writing back to agent memory) is a plausible real-world attack vector, not just a theoretical one
- Speed of the cyber validation loop: 2–3 weeks from hypothesis → research → real breach demo → CISO conversation is the expected minimum cycle time; plan for this pace, not months
