## 2026-06-22 - Meeting: Cyber Ideation Kickoff — Agent-to-Agent Security Direction
- "Agent-to-agent security" (security for communication between AI agents across different apps/processes in an enterprise) is a potentially unoccupied space — NHIs and AI DLP are already crowded, but inter-app A2A comms security has no clear dominant player yet
- Conviction on a domain is not enough; investors (and founders) need conviction on a *specific problem* within the domain — Avishay's articulation: "I need conviction on the problem, not just the space"
- Building domain feel requires conversations with practitioners, not just reading — theoretical understanding of CISO personas is insufficient; need lived exposure to what actually keeps them up at night
- A 2-week discovery sprint with daily check-ins is a viable structure for building cyber conviction or ruling it out — long enough for signal, short enough to not get sucked in without a decision gate
- The "bottom-up" AI security sell (employee AI tool adoption → CISO risk surface) may be more accessible than the top-down CISO motion for founders without enterprise security backgrounds
- AI Security already has explicit budget lines at many enterprises — framing matters: labeling a product "AI Security" unlocks existing procurement categories that would otherwise require education
- Agent-to-agent comms in enterprises today are mostly custom-built in-house; products like Claude Code spawn sub-agents but within a single identity/permission boundary — true cross-app agent communication is still emerging
- Keeping a low-cost product option alive (Tranzila at 180 ILS/month) while pivoting focus is a legitimate strategy for preserving optionality without real distraction cost
- Different time zones between co-founders is a real multiplier killer — proximity (even virtual daily syncs) meaningfully reduces coordination friction and loss of shared momentum
