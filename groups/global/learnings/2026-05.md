## 2026-05-06 - Meeting: Creative Analysis Agent — Experiment Planning

- **Low-ticket + new opportunity framing lowers the trust bar**: For products under ~$50, skepticism about social proof matters less because the downside risk is minimal; framing the mechanism as a "new opportunity" (distinct from everything they've tried) also removes the comparison to past failures
- **Higher-awareness targeting converts better with static creatives**: Talking to D2C brands in the language of "your creative isn't converting" (not "your ads don't work") addresses a more aware segment who don't need educating — static ads work for them
- **Meta Advantage+ makes manual targeting redundant**: Traditional audience targeting on Meta is effectively obsolete; Advantage+ handles distribution, so the creative itself becomes the primary lever
- **Fake checkout as demand-proxy is a valid MVP signal**: Collecting email + showing a "proceed to payment" CTA without a live payment flow is sufficient to measure real purchase intent before building the full product
- **ChatGPT gpt-image-1 now leads for realistic UGC-style ad creatives**: As of May 2026, ChatGPT's image model outperforms Gemini/Imagen for photorealistic, human-in-scene static ads; character consistency can be maintained by re-uploading the image in a fresh session
- **Agent questionnaire design matters more than live report generation**: For the pilot, the value is in the quality of the diagnostic questions — the actual report can be delivered manually; this decouples the AI-facing UX from back-end complexity

## 2026-05-06 - Meeting: Runway Chat (onboarding process)

- Time-to-value is the north star metric for B2B onboarding; mapping the funnel from sign-up to first key action is the prerequisite before designing any playbook.

## 2026-05-05 - Meeting: Dean Greenberg & Keshet Tavor (Google retail)

- Grocery is the natural first wedge for conversational AI commerce: high frequency, low deliberation, strong habit loops. Unlike brand retail (Nike shoes), users have a pre-existing relationship with *their* supermarket, making an AI agent feel useful rather than intrusive.
- Attribution is the product for B2B AI-visibility tools: impressions in GMC are not a strong enough KPI — sophisticated buyers need to see revenue movement (basket additions, conversion rate). Until that signal exists, the value prop remains speculative.
- Ask pilot merchants what they'd pay for *before* presenting results — surfaces real success criteria and prevents building toward metrics the customer doesn't care about.
- Competitive whitespace research is a forcing function: asking competitors "why aren't you doing this?" surfaces real blockers (technical, commercial, regulatory) far faster than internal analysis.
- Feed optimization and AI storefronts are complementary but distinct value propositions; conflating them in the pitch creates confusion. Separate them clearly for each audience.
