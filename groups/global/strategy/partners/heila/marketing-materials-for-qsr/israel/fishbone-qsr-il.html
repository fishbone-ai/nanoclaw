Why QSR

QSR diners arrive in chat with intent, not a search query: hungry now, a familiar order in mind, a quick decision. The AI compresses in one turn what most apps stretch across screens - remembered favorites, combos and sides, local promotions, pickup vs delivery. That's why the first wave of consumer AI ordering apps in 2026 is QSR-led.

Fishbone

Connecting QSR brands' ordering ecosystems to the AI platforms consumers consolidate around.

Consumers increasingly use AI assistants as personal butlers, for planning, research, and ordering. The trend is consolidation toward these platforms. Fishbone connects a QSR brand's menu, voice, and ordering stack into this shift so they show up where customers are already there, enabling everything from menu discovery and personalization to cart construction to checkout, all on top of the brand's existing infrastructure. Nothing gets replaced. The QSR brand stays in control.
